Demo / educational only. Not investment advice. Data is delayed, indicative or end-of-day and may be wrong or stale; each panel's badge shows its source, as-of time and whether it is live, cached or a snapshot. Equity prices are end-of-day snapshots, never real-time; if a snapshot says SIMULATED it is not market data. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any data provider. Data from CoinGecko, Binance public market data, Frankfurter/ECB, US Treasury, FRED, Federal Reserve, ECB and SEC — see the README for terms.

Shortcuts: Ctrl/⌘ K palette · Alt 1–9 jump to panel · drag headers to move, corner to resize (or focus a header, arrows move, Shift+arrows resize).
