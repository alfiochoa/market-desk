Demo / educational only. Not investment advice. Data is delayed, indicative or end-of-day and may be wrong or stale; each panel's badge shows its source, as-of time and whether it is live, cached or a snapshot. Equities (Twelve Data, free shared key): end-of-day, refreshed daily after the US close, plus delayed intraday quotes for a few priority symbols about every 30–60 min in US market hours (best-effort). Never real-time. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any data provider. Crypto prices powered by CoinGecko; also Binance public market data, Frankfurter/ECB, US Treasury, FRED, Federal Reserve, ECB and SEC — see the README for terms.

Shortcuts: Ctrl/⌘ K palette · Alt 1–9 jump to panel · drag headers to move, corner to resize (or focus a header, arrows move, Shift+arrows resize).
